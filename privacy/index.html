Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

L.A. Builds respects your privacy. This policy describes the information provided through our website and project inquiries, how it is used, and your choices.

Information you provide

When you contact us, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, property location, project details and message. If you separately choose to receive text messages, we record your consent and any later opt-out request.

How information is used

Inquiry information is used to respond to requests, discuss project scope, prepare estimates, coordinate appointments and communicate about projects. SMS messages are sent only when you have opted in to the described program. We do not sell personal information.

Mobile information & SMS consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase or submitting an inquiry. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Read the SMS program terms for more details.

Other information and service providers

Non-mobile inquiry information may be processed by service providers solely to support our business communications and project administration. It may also be disclosed when required by law. These uses do not authorize sharing mobile information for marketing or sharing text messaging opt-in data or consent with third parties.

Website tools

The contact page embeds our inquiry form through LeadConnector (GoHighLevel). That service may process inquiry details and technical information needed to operate the form. This site does not add separate analytics or advertising scripts.

Cookies & tracking

This site does not use its own analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The inquiry form on our contact page is provided by LeadConnector (GoHighLevel), which may set cookies or use similar technologies needed to load the form, prevent spam and record your submission. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some form features may not work without them.

Retention, security & your choices

We retain information as needed to respond to inquiries, administer projects, honor opt-out requests and meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information; no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Contact & updates

For privacy questions, contact L.A. Builds at business@labuilds.co, (314) 548-0306, or by mail at 318 Greystone Ave, Palmyra, MO 63461. Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
